SSC Stenographer Grade 'C' & 'D' Master Guide: 2026 Edition

1. SELECTION PROCESS (NO MATHS EXAM)

The SSC Stenographer recruitment is a unique two-stage process designed for those who have a strong command of languages. Since there is no Quantitative Aptitude (Maths) section, the final merit is determined solely by your CBT score, provided you pass the mandatory Shorthand Skill Test.

2. COMPUTER BASED TEST (CBT) PATTERN

You have 120 minutes (2 hours) to solve 200 questions. Accuracy is key as there is a negative marking of **0.25 marks** for every incorrect response.

5. EVALUATION OF TRANSCRIPTION (MISTAKE RULES)

The SSC follows strict rules for checking Shorthand scripts. **Full Mistakes** include omitting words or substituting words. **Half Mistakes** include spelling errors and capitalization errors.

SARKARKINOKRI "TRUSTED ADVISOR" STENO INSIGHTS

  • English is the King: Since 50% of the paper (100 marks) is English, focus heavily on Active/Passive and Direct/Indirect speech. These 20-25 marks are pure logic and very scoring.
  • The Shorthand Reality: Shorthand cannot be learned in a month. If you are waiting for the CBT results to start learning Steno, you will fail the Skill Test. Start now.
  • Audio Clarity: During the actual Skill Test, you will get two 1-minute trial passages. Use this to check the speaker volume—if it's not clear, raise your hand immediately!
  • Transcription Tip: Always finish typing 5-10 minutes early to check for "Half Mistakes" like commas and full stops which can add up and lead to disqualification.
