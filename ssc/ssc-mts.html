SARKARKINOKRI "TRUSTED ADVISOR" MTS STRATEGY

  • The Session-I Logic: Don't waste 90% of your time on Maths and Reasoning. Since there is no negative marking and it's only qualifying, focus just enough to pass.
  • The Real Game: Your entire career depends on **General Awareness and English**. These 50 questions in Session-II are the only things that matter for the Merit List.
  • Safe Score: Aim for 120+ out of 150 in Session-II to secure a seat in X-category cities (Delhi, Mumbai, etc.).
