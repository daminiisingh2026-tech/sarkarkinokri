SARKARKINOKRI "TRUSTED ADVISOR" JE INSIGHTS

  • The Paper-II Shift: Remember that Paper-II is no longer conventional (subjective). It is now **Objective (CBT)**. Practice numerically heavy MCQ questions using a calculator.
  • Medical Standards: BRO (Border Roads Organization) has very strict physical and medical standards. If you are not medically fit as per their manual, do not put them as your first preference.
  • Non-Tech Weightage: In Paper-I, 100 marks are from Reasoning and GA. Many engineers fail here despite technical brilliance. Don't ignore the 50 marks of General Awareness.
