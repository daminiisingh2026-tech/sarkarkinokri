SARKARKINOKRI "TRUSTED ADVISOR" INSIGHTS

  • Merit Strategy: Section II (English & GA) has the highest weightage (180 Marks). Do not treat GA as a secondary subject.
  • The Computer Barrier: Even if you score 300+ in Section I & II, you will be disqualified if you fail to meet the qualifying marks in the Computer Module.
  • Typing Consistency: Many aspirants clear the CBT but fail the Typing test. Start practicing on a physical keyboard 20 minutes daily from today.
