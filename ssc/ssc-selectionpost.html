Unlike CGL or CHSL, Selection Posts are categorized by your educational qualification. You must appear for the exam level corresponding to the post you applied for.

3. POST-EXAM SCRUTINY (THE MOST CRITICAL STAGE)

Selection Posts have a unique "Scrutiny" phase. After the written exam, candidates in the merit list must send hard copies of their documents to the Regional Office.

SARKARKINOKRI "TRUSTED ADVISOR" SELECTION POST TIPS

  • Multiple Applications: You can apply for multiple posts across different levels. If you apply for a 10th-level post and a Graduate-level post, you must appear for TWO separate exams.
  • Experience Certificates: 70% of rejections in Selection Posts happen during "Scrutiny." Ensure your experience certificate matches the **exact work description** mentioned in the official notification.
  • EQ Cut-off Date: Your degree or experience must be completed *before* the closing date of the application. Back-dated certificates are strictly scrutinized.
