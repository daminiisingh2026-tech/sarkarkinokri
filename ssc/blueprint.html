SSC Preparation Blueprint: The 90-Day Success Path

Success in SSC CGL, CHSL, or MTS isn't about studying 15 hours a day; it's about studying the right topics in the right order. Below is our expert-verified roadmap for 2026.

🚀 Phase 1: Concept Mastery (Day 1 - 30)

Focus on clearing concepts. Do not touch mock tests yet. Mastery of calculation techniques and English Grammar rules is the priority.

  • Maths: Percentages, Ratio, and basic Algebra.
  • English: 12 Tenses, Active/Passive, and basic Vocab (50 words/day).
  • Reasoning: Series and Coding-Decoding.
TARGET: Complete 60% of the syllabus & prepare short-formula hand-written notes.

⏰ THE EXPERT'S DAILY SCHEDULE

06:00 AM - 08:00 AMGeneral Awareness & Current Affairs
09:00 AM - 12:00 PMMathematics (Concept + Practice)
02:00 PM - 04:00 PMEnglish Grammar & Vocabulary
05:00 PM - 07:00 PMReasoning & Previous Year Analysis
09:00 PM - 10:00 PMDaily Revision (Non-Negotiable)
⚙️ Phase 2: Speed & Accuracy (Day 31 - 60)

Start solving Chapter-wise Previous Year Questions (PYQs). Use the 5-year trend analysis to prioritize topics.

  • Maths: Geometry, Mensuration, and Trigonometry shortcuts.
  • English: Reading Comprehension speed & Cloze Test patterns.
  • GA: Focus on Static GK (Dance, Festivals, Articles, Minerals).
TARGET: Solve 100 questions per subject daily with a timer.
🏆 Phase 3: The Mock Marathon (Day 61 - 90)

One Full-Length Mock Test every morning. Analyze the mistakes for 3 hours. This phase decides your selection.

  • Analysis: Focus on 'Time Taken' vs 'Accuracy' for every question.
  • Revision: Revise your Phase 1 notes every 3 days.
  • Typing: Start 30 minutes of typing practice daily.
TARGET: Stabilize score in the top 5 percentile of mock takers.

SSC "CHEAT SHEET": SUBJECT-WISE WEIGHTAGE

SubjectHigh-Yield TopicsExpert Strategy
Maths Geometry, Data Interpretation, Algebra Focus on 2D/3D Mensuration formulas; SSC repeats these every year.
English Cloze Test, Narration, Error Spotting Read an Editorial daily for 30 mins to improve comprehension speed.
GA Polity (Articles), Modern History, Science Do not read random news; stick to last 8 months of Monthly Magazines.

SARKARKINOKRI "TRUSTED ADVISOR" GOLDEN RULES

  • The 5-Year Law: SSC repeats 40-50% of concepts from PYQs. Solve papers from 2019 to 2024 before touching any other book.
  • Computer Awareness: Many high scorers fail because they ignore the Computer module. Spend 1 hour every Sunday on MS Office and Internet basics.
  • Analysis is Key: Giving a mock test without 3 hours of analysis is a waste of time. Track why you got a question wrong: Silly Mistake, Concept Gap, or Time Pressure?
