Railway 7th CPC Salary Structure

Official Earnings, Perks, and Deduction Breakdown for 2026

1. Post-Wise Pay Level Matrix

Your "Basic Pay" is determined by your Pay Level. As of 2026, the DA (Dearness Allowance) is expected to be over 50%, significantly boosting the gross salary.

2. City-Wise House Rent Allowance (HRA)

The HRA percentage depends entirely on the population of the city where you are posted:

Class X (27-30%)
Metro Cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad.
Class Y (18-20%)
Tier-2 Cities: Patna, Lucknow, Jaipur, Pune, Ahmedabad, Nagpur.
Class Z (9-10%)
Village/Small Towns: All other smaller stations and rural postings.

3. Perks & Special Allowances

Running Allowance (KMA) - Exclusive to ALP/Guards

Calculated per 100km of travel. For an ALP, this can add ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 per month to the salary.

NDA: Night Duty Allowance for shifts between 10 PM and 6 AM.
NHA: National Holiday Allowance for working on gazetted holidays.
WA: Washing Allowance for maintenance of official uniform.
TA: Transport Allowance to cover daily commuting costs.

4. Monthly Deductions & Net Pay

Your "In-Hand" salary is the Gross Salary minus the following statutory deductions:

  • NPS (National Pension System): 10% of (Basic + DA). Government contributes 14%.
  • CGHS / RELHS: Monthly contribution for Railway medical facilities.
  • Professional Tax: Varies by state (usually ₹200).
  • Insurance (GIS): A small monthly premium for group insurance cover.
