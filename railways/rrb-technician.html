RRB Technician 2026: Expanded Selection Blueprint

1. The Eligibility Pillar: Grade I vs Grade III

Technician 2026 recruitment is divided into two distinct levels. Candidates with B.E/B.Tech/B.Sc usually opt for Grade I, while ITI holders focus on Grade III.

Signal Department
Grade I (Signal)

Pay Level: Level 5 (7th CPC)

Education: B.Sc in Physics/Electronics/Computer Science/IT OR B.E./B.Tech in Instrumentation/3-Year Diploma.

Core Focus: Digital Electronics, Computer Networking, and Measurement Laws.

Workshop/P-Way
Grade III (Various Trades)

Pay Level: Level 2 (7th CPC)

Education: Matriculation (10th) + ITI from NCVT/SCVT in relevant trades (Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Machinist, etc.).

Core Focus: Trade-specific tools, safety protocols, and foundational Science.

2. Technical Syllabus Breakdown (CBT Only)

Note: There is NO interview. Your CBT score is 100% of your merit. Below is the detailed topic list:

General Awareness & Reasoning (Standard)

  • Current Affairs: Science & Tech, Sports, Culture, Personalities, Economics, Politics.
  • Reasoning: Analogies, Alphabetical & Number Series, Coding-Decoding, Mathematical operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagram, Data Interpretation and Sufficiency.

Grade I Special: Basics of Computers & IT

Architecture of Computers, Input & Output devices, Storage devices, Networking, Operating Systems (Windows/Unix/Linux), MS Office, Data Representation (Binary/Hex), Internet and Email, Computer Virus.

Grade I Special: Basic Science & Engineering (Physics)

Units and Measurements, Mass, Weight and Density, Work, Power and Energy, Speed and Velocity, Heat and Temperature, Basic Electricity, Levers and Simple Machines, Occupational Safety and Health, Environmental Education, IT Literacy.

Grade III Special: Mathematics & Science Focus

  • Math: Number system, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics.
  • General Science: Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences (10th Standard CBSE Level).

3. Expert Zone Selection Strategy

Zone selection is the "Silent Filter" in Railway exams. Choose wisely:

  • High Cut-off Zones: Allahabad (NCR), Chandigarh (NR), Patna (ECR).
  • Moderate Cut-off Zones: Mumbai (CR/WR), Secunderabad (SCR), Kolkata (ER/SER).
  • Low Cut-off Zones: Malda, Siliguri, Guwahati (NFR).

Pro-Tip: Check the "Category-wise Vacancy" for your specific trade (e.g., Fitter) before applying. Do not just look at the total vacancy.

4. Medical Classifications for Technicians

B-1 Standard (Workshop/Signal): 6/9, 6/12 with or without glasses (Power not to exceed 4D). Must pass Color Vision, Binocular Vision, and Night Vision tests.

C-1 Standard (Clerical/Technical Assist): 6/12, 6/18 with or without glasses. Near Vision: 0.6, 0.6 with or without glasses while reading or doing close work.
