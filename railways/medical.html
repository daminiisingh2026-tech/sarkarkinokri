Selection Criteria for Group 'C' & 'D' Technical/Non-Technical Posts

2. The Railway Medical Examination (RME) Tests

Candidates must undergo a battery of physical tests at a designated Railway Hospital. Failure in any single test may result in a lower medical classification.

Vision Tests

Color Blindness: Tested via Ishihara Plates. Binocular Vision: Ability to use both eyes together. Night Vision: Testing for Nyctalopia.

Physical Tests

Diabetes/Urine: Routine check for sugar and albumin. Blood Pressure: To ensure no hypertension. Chest X-Ray: To check for TB or respiratory issues.

Ear & Surgical

Hearing: Tested at a distance of 6 meters. Hydrocele/Hernia: Candidates with active conditions may be given time for surgery and re-examination.

3. Medical Re-Appeal & Disqualification Guide

If you are found medically unfit for the post you were selected for, you have one chance to appeal. This process is strictly governed by the following rules:

  • Appeal Duration: Usually within 30 days of the medical result announcement.
  • Private Certificate: You must obtain a certificate from a Specialist (Government or Private) stating the Railway's diagnosis is incorrect.
  • The Fee: A nominal re-appeal fee (approx ₹1000) must be paid via Demand Draft to the PCMD (Principal Chief Medical Director).
  • Final Verdict: The decision of the Three-Member Medical Board at the Zonal Railway Hospital is final.
