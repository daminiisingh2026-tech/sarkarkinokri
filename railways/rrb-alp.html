RRB ALP 2026: The Assistant Loco Pilot Protocol

1. The 3-Tier Selection Architecture

Unlike other exams, ALP requires a blend of speed (CBT), technical accuracy (Trade Test), and psychological stability (CBAT).

CBT-1 (Screening): 60 Minutes | 75 Questions.
Focus: Math (20), Reasoning (25), Science (20), GA (10).
CBT-2 (The Decider): 2 Hours 30 Minutes.
• Part A (100 Qs): Math, Reasoning, Basic Science & Engineering. (Determines Merit)
• Part B (75 Qs): Relevant Trade (Electrician/Fitter/etc). (Qualifying only - 35% needed)
CBAT (The Psycho Test): 5 Battery Tests. No negative marking. Requires high T-Score (42 in each battery).

Final Merit Calculation Formula

70% (CBT-2 Part A) + 30% (CBAT Score)

Note: Part B Trade test is mandatory but marks are not added to merit.

2. Basic Science & Engineering (The Game Changer)

This section in CBT-2 Part A carries the highest weightage for technical candidates.

Engineering Drawing

Projections, Views, Drawing Instruments, Lines, Geometric Figures, Symbolic Representation.

Physics & Units

Measurements, Mass, Weight and Density, Work Power and Energy, Speed and Velocity, Heat and Temperature.

Basic Electricity

Levers and Simple Machines, Occupational Safety and Health, IT Literacy.

General Intelligence

Analogies, Syllogism, Venn Diagram, Data Interpretation, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding.

⚠️ The A-1 Medical "Hard-Stop"

ALP candidates must meet the A-1 Medical Standard. This is non-negotiable:

  • Vision: 6/6, 6/6 without glasses (No Fogging Test permitted).
  • Near Vision: 0.6, 0.6 without glasses.
  • Color Vision: Must pass Color Blindness, Binocular Vision, Field of Vision, and Night Vision tests.
  • LASIK: Any history of refractive surgery (LASIK/ICL) results in instant permanent disqualification.
