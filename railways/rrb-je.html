RRB JE 2026: Engineering Selection Protocol

⚙️ Senior Section Engineer (SSE) & JE Note: Unlike NTPC, the JE exam requires a dual-brain approach. CBT-1 is purely for speed (Non-Tech), while CBT-2 is for core technical depth. If you neglect Non-Tech, you will never reach the Technical round.

1. The Two-Stage Selection Matrix

Feature CBT Stage 1 CBT Stage 2
Purpose Screening/Elimination Final Merit Ranking
Duration 90 Minutes 120 Minutes

Technical Weight 0% (Pure Non-Tech) 66.6% (100 Marks)

2. Technical Discipline Allotment

Candidates are grouped into "Exam Groups" based on their degree/diploma. You will only solve technical questions from your specific group:

Civil & Allied Group

Construction, Estimating, Costing & Valuation, Surveying, Soil Mechanics, Hydraulics, Irrigation Engineering.

Electrical & Allied Group

Basic concepts, Circuit law, Magnetic Circuit, AC Fundamentals, Electrical Machines, Synchronous Machines.

Mechanical & Allied Group

Engineering Mechanics, Material Science, Strength of Materials, Machining, Welding, Grinding & Finishing.

Electronics & Allied Group

Electronic Devices, Analog Circuits, Digital Electronics, Microprocessor & Microcontroller, Data Communication.

🚨 IMPORTANT: A virtual on-screen calculator will be provided ONLY during CBT Stage 2. Physical calculators are strictly prohibited. Practice using the mouse for calculations during your mock tests.

3. Detailed Syllabus: CBT-2 Non-Tech Portion (50 Marks)

While the technical part is 100 marks, these 50 marks usually decide the "Top Zone" merit:

  • General Awareness (15 Marks): Focus on Current Affairs, Geography, Culture and History of India including freedom struggle, Environmental issues, and Indian Economy.
  • Physics & Chemistry (15 Marks): Up to 10th Standard CBSE syllabus. Focus on Chemical Reactions and Physics Numerical problems.
  • Basics of Computers (10 Marks): Architecture, Input & Output devices, Networking, Operating Systems (Windows/Unix/Linux), MS Office, Data Representation, Internet and Email.
  • Basics of Environment & Pollution Control (10 Marks): Basics of Environment; Adverse effect of environmental pollution and control strategies; Air, water and Noise pollution, Waste Management, Global warming; Acid rain; Ozone depletion.

4. Junior Engineer Medical Standards

Category A-3: Physically fit in all respects. Visual Standards—Distance Vision: 6/9, 6/9 with or without glasses (Power of lenses not to exceed 2D). Near Vision: 0.6, 0.6 with or without glasses.

Salary Profile: Pay Level 6 (7th CPC). Basic Pay: ₹35,400 + DA + HRA. Gross Salary starts approx ₹55,000 - ₹62,000 depending on city (X, Y, Z).
