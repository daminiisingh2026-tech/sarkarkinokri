2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) Master Guide

Passing the CBT is not enough. You must qualify the PET, which is mandatory but qualifying in nature. There are no marks for PET.

♂️ For Male Candidates:
  • Weight Carry: Lift and carry 35 kg of weight for a distance of 100 meters in 2 minutes in one chance without putting weight down.
  • Running: Run for a distance of 1000 meters in 4 minutes and 15 seconds in one chance.
♀️ For Female/Transgender:
  • Weight Carry: Lift and carry 20 kg of weight for a distance of 100 meters in 2 minutes in one chance without putting weight down.
  • Running: Run for a distance of 1000 meters in 5 minutes and 40 seconds in one chance.
3. Expanded CBT Syllabus & Subject Strategy

🧪 General Science (25 Marks)

Focus: Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences of 10th standard level (CBSE). Focus heavily on **Chemical Reactions, Periodic Table, Light (Reflection/Refraction), and Human Anatomy**.

🔢 Mathematics (25 Marks)

Focus: Number system, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry.

🧠 General Intelligence & Reasoning (30 Marks)

Focus: This is the scoring section. Focus on **Syllogism, Venn Diagrams, Mathematical Operations, and Data Interpretation**. Expect tricky coding-decoding questions.

🌍 General Awareness & Current Affairs (20 Marks)

Focus: Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Personalities, Economics, Politics and any other subject of importance. Focus on the last 12 months of Railway-specific news.

4. Normalization & Merit Rules

Since Group D is conducted in multiple shifts, **Percentile Based Normalization** is applied. Your raw marks will be converted into a percentile score based on the performance of other candidates in your specific shift.

  • Negative Marking: 1/3rd mark deducted for every wrong answer.
  • Minimum Pass %: UR-40%, EWS-40%, OBC-30%, SC-30%, ST-25%.
